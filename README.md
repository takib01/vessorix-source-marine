--------------------------------------------------
OUR BUSINESS MODEL
--------------------------------------------------

Vessorix is NOT a traditional ecommerce store.

Customers normally have a specific marine equipment requirement.

They may send:
- Maker / brand
- Model
- Part number
- Equipment photo
- Nameplate photo
- Technical specification
- Quantity
- Vessel information
- RFQ document

Vessorix then sources the requested marine spare/equipment and sends a quotation containing:

- Availability
- Condition
- Price
- Lead time
- Shipping options

Therefore, the MAIN conversion action everywhere on the website should be:

“Send Your RFQ”

Secondary CTAs:
“Request a Quote”
“Send Your Requirement”
“Contact Sales”

Do NOT build a shopping cart or online checkout.

IMPORTANT BUSINESS CLAIM RULES
--------------------------------------------------

Do NOT falsely claim:

- “All products are in stock”
- “Ready stock”
- “Authorized distributor”
- “Official dealer”
- “OEM authorized”
- “100% genuine”

- fake projects
- fake certifications
- fake testimonials

We are primarily a SOURCING AND SUPPLY business.

Use language such as:

“We source marine automation, electrical, instrumentation and engine-control spare parts based on customer requirements.”

“If you have a current requirement, send us the maker, model, part number, photo or RFQ.”

“We will respond with availability, condition, price, lead time and shipping options.”

“We aim to provide highly competitive sourcing for marine automation and spare parts.”

“Condition and test status are clearly stated with each quotation.”
